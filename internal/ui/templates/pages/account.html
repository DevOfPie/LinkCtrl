Bot blocking

Automated clients asking for a short link on {{.BotHost}} can be refused with a 403 instead of being redirected. Every link follows this unless its owner says otherwise; enforcing it takes that choice away from them.

Detection is the same user-agent heuristic the click statistics use. It treats a missing user agent as a bot and matches on substrings including preview, monitor and checker, and its false-positive rate has never been measured. A person it misjudges gets the 403 with no way past it and no notice to the link's owner.

Delete this account

Permanent, and it takes effect the moment you confirm. Your sessions, API keys, notifications and memberships go with it, and the address becomes available for a new account. What other people can still see — the audit trail, and any destination review you filed or decided — stays, because it is a record of what happened rather than a record of you; your name and address are scrubbed out of it by a job that runs every hour.
