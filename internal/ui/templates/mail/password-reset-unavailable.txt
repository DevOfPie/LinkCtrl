Hello,

Somebody asked to reset the password for this address ({{.Email}}) on this
LinkCtrl instance. No reset link was created and nothing was changed.

There are two reasons that happens, and this message deliberately does not say
which: either the address has no account here, or the account it has cannot be
recovered by password reset. Whoever runs this instance is the only person who
can tell you which, and the only person who can do anything about the second.

If it was not you, you can ignore this. Somebody typed your address into a
reset form and learned nothing: the form answers the same way whatever the
address is, which is why this message exists.
