# M49 — QR codes sized in pixels, and a PNG to download

**Depends on:** [M48](m48.md), and the edge is an *ordering preference* that is
nearly hard. The QR panel is `internal/ui/templates/partials/link_qr.html` (171 lines) plus
the panel body at `pages/link_qr.html`, which [M46](m46.md)–[M48](m48.md) have
now restructured; landing a settings rewrite into a page being rebuilt would have
meant writing it twice. **This milestone is not a fallback
destination for a stalled B**, and an earlier draft of this file said it was:
[W33](../workflow-changes.md#made)'s fallback requires *every* row in a
milestone's `Depends on` column to be `done`, so a blocked M48 blocks M49 too.
The phase's actual fallbacks from a stalled B are [M51](m51.md) and
[M55](m55.md), whose dependency columns are empty.
**Discharges:** Plan.md's *Not in Phase 2* row **A PNG QR code**, and the third
of the three redesign complaints in
[phase-3-candidates.md](../phase-3-candidates.md)'s area B — *the QR settings
expose Quiet zone and Module size where an end user wants output size in pixels*.
It reverses **[D11](../../../Plan.md#phase-2-decisions)**.

The QR form asks the person printing a poster for a quiet zone in modules and a
module size in pixels. Nobody printing a poster knows either number. This
milestone replaces both with the number they do know — how big they want it —
and gives them a file they can use, which today they cannot get without saving
an SVG and finding something to convert it.

## Done means

### The vocabulary

- The form's size control is **one number: output size in pixels.** Quiet zone
  and module size leave the interface entirely; they stay as the arithmetic
  behind it.
- Colours stay — foreground and background are a choice a person makes and can
  see the result of.
- **Error-correction level leaves the UI and stays in the API.** It is a
  scannability tradeoff that a dashboard user has no basis to make and a script
  might. The split is deliberate and is the milestone's answer to *"the rest
  handled in the background"*: background means chosen well, not made
  unreachable.
- `qr.Style` keeps `Margin`, `Scale` and `Level` as the generator's parameters.
  The change is what the *surface* asks for, not what the renderer accepts.

### The size is snapped, and the interface says so

> *(**This whole subsection is superseded**, 2026-08-13, at the second
> reopening — D182. Nothing snaps: the requested size is exact at every value,
> and there is no reported pair because there is no discrepancy to report. It is
> left standing rather than rewritten because it is the record of what shipped
> and of what the owner then rejected, and because the paragraph immediately
> below is the sharpest thing in this file — it files the answer that eventually
> shipped under *rejected*, and the reason it gives is **not** the reason D182
> found. Marked once here rather than six times inside; every sentence below is
> past tense as of this note.)*

A QR grid is a whole number of modules, so an arbitrary pixel size does not
divide evenly — 300px over 29 modules is 10.34 pixels per module. This milestone
**snaps to the nearest size that keeps modules whole** and reports the size it
actually produced beside the one that was asked for.

The alternative — honour the requested size exactly and let modules land on
fractional boundaries — is recorded and rejected here: it makes the SVG and the
PNG round differently, which is precisely the claim below that must hold.

> *(**And that rejection was right about its own alternative and wrong about the
> space.** Fractional module boundaries would indeed make the two encoders
> disagree, and D182 does not do that: the **symbol** still lands on whole
> modules and the two encoders still round identically. What D182 changes is the
> **quiet zone**, which is white space and needs no grid at all — a third option
> neither this paragraph nor D179 considered. `size = modules·scale +
> 2·margin_px`. The claim below still holds and is still asserted.)*

- The snap is asserted by test across the module counts the content lengths in
  this product actually produce.
- The quiet zone stays at or above the 4 modules the specification requires. It
  is derived, never below the floor, and a test asserts the floor across the
  size range. *(Amended 2026-08-13 at the second reopening: the floor is **three**
  modules since D182, which is below what the specification requires and is
  measured through `make verify-scan` rather than assumed. `MinMarginModules = 3`
  is what a test now asserts across the range.)*

### The claim that the two outputs match

**A test asserts the SVG and the PNG describe the same image at the same
requested size.** This is the owner's expectation stated as a mechanism:

- Both are generated from the same module matrix, the same snapped
  pixels-per-module and the same origin offset — one arithmetic, two encoders,
  asserted by construction rather than by eye.
- The test parses the SVG's rects and, for every module in the grid, checks that
  the pixel at that module's centre in the PNG carries the colour the SVG's
  coverage implies. Foreground and background both. It fails if either encoder
  drifts.
- The SVG is emitted with explicit pixel `width` and `height` rather than
  relying on a viewBox alone, so *rendered at the set pixel size* is what a
  browser actually does with it.

Stating the limit as well: this asserts the **module geometry** matches. It does
not assert a rasteriser's antialiasing of the SVG is byte-identical to the PNG,
which is not true of any two rasterisers and is not what was asked for.

### PNG, and why D11 reverses cleanly

- `image/png` from the standard library. **No new module dependency**, asserted
  by a test on `go.mod`'s require block.
- D11 said SVG only *because no image encoder was in the dependency set and
  nothing should rasterise on a request*.
  [D72](../../../Plan.md#phase-2-decisions-taken-after-the-plan-was-finalised)
  sharpened it, rejecting a QR library for pulling `image/png` and
  `compress/zlib` in *"for a PNG path this product will never call"*. The
  premise of both is that the path is never called. This milestone makes it
  called, on request, by a person who asked for a file — so the reversal honours
  the reasoning rather than overriding it. The package doc at
  `internal/qr/qr.go:3-5` already anticipated it: *"A PNG download, if it is ever
  wanted, is an additive change here and nowhere else."*
- **Rasterising on a request gets a bound, because that is what D11 was
  protecting.** Output size is capped, the cap is stated as a number with the
  resulting maximum allocation, and a request above it is refused rather than
  clamped. The QR endpoints are dashboard and API surfaces, not the redirect
  path, so no SLO re-verification is owed — said explicitly so the omission is
  visible.
- The PNG endpoint carries the same caching treatment the SVG has
  (`internal/httpx/api_qr.go:33`), and a download disposition, because a file
  somebody can save is the point.

### Stored styles keep working

`qr_codes.style` is a jsonb blob holding a marshalled `qr.Style`
(`00600_phase2_dormant.sql:60`, `internal/link/qr.go:118`). Rows written before
this milestone carry `Margin` and `Scale` and no size.

- They are read forward: an existing style resolves to the output size its
  margin and scale already produce, so no stored code changes appearance.
  Asserted by a test that writes a pre-milestone style, reads it after, and
  compares the rendered bytes.
- **The demo already carries a stored style, so it exercises this path on the
  day the milestone lands.** `cmd/lctl/demo_phase2.go` seeds one
  (`Level: qr.LevelQ, Margin: 4, Scale: 10`), and `demoCoverage()`'s M41 row *A
  QR code somebody has styled* (`cmd/lctl/demo_coverage_test.go:522`) requires
  it. *(Corrected 2026-08-07, from review. This bullet read "The demo has no
  stored QR styles today — every panel renders at defaults", which was drawn
  from a query over the twelve oldest links and generalised to the table. The
  demo holds two `qr_codes` rows. The correction matters: the read-forward
  behaviour is demonstrated rather than only asserted, and that is a stronger
  position than the one the file claimed.)*

### Demo

**No new `demoCoverage()` row.** The M41 row above already requires a styled
code and is bounded `Min: 1, Max: 1`, so a second row asserting the same table
would either duplicate it or contradict its ceiling. What this milestone changes
is the *seeded style itself*: it carries an explicit output size, so the demo
shows a sized code and a working PNG download rather than a default nobody
chose. If that turns out to need its own row, it amends the M41 row rather than
sitting beside it — and the milestone records which it did.

## Risks

**Low to moderate.** The generator is 395 lines of hand-rolled SVG with a test
file longer still, and the module matrix it draws from is unchanged — PNG is a
second encoder over the same data.

The real risk is the snap. It is a small arithmetic change that silently affects
every rendered code, and *nearest size that keeps modules whole* has edge cases
at both ends of the range and at content lengths that push the code into a
larger version. The test is written across the range before the snap is, not
after.

The unknown is whether *output size in pixels* is what a person printing a poster
actually wants, or whether they want millimetres at a stated DPI. This milestone
builds what was asked for; if the answer turns out to be print units, that is a
label and a conversion over the same arithmetic rather than a rework.

# Reopened — 2026-08-12

**Three of this milestone's surfaces did not survive the owner's use**
([F213](../deferred-findings.md#open)). Work below carries M49's number.
Reviewed by [M57.9](m57.9.md) — M49 sits inside its range.

## Done means, as reopened

- **The preview keeps a fixed footprint.** Applying a size changes the
  stored size — what the download and the API serve — while the on-page
  preview scales *down* to a stated maximum box and never grows the page.
  Asserted by a template test on the preview's declared bounds, and the
  stored-vs-drawn distinction is stated where the preview renders.
- **The quiet zone is the minimum that scans.** The ISO floor is 4 modules
  (`DefaultMargin`); whatever `qr.FitSize` currently does between a
  requested pixel size and the module grid is pinned first, then bounded:
  at every acceptable size the majority of the image is code and the
  margin does not grow past the floor by more than the remainder a module
  grid forces. *(Amended 2026-08-13 at the second reopening: **both halves
  are given up**, and deliberately. D182 makes the requested size exact, and
  an exact size is not simultaneously satisfiable with a majority-code
  guarantee — an 89-module code at 256px has one admissible scale and comes
  out 48% code. The margin is no longer pinned at the floor either; it lands
  in 3-5 modules where an integer scale admits it and errs wide where none
  does. Given up on the record rather than quietly broken.)* `MaxMargin = 16` is revisited against that bound and the
  outcome recorded. A style stored under the old arithmetic keeps
  rendering; if its drawn margin changes, that is stated, not silent.
  Asserted in `internal/qr`'s own tests across the size range, including
  2000px, where the owner saw it worst.
- **The reset button reads "Restore defaults".** One label
  (`link_qr.html:278` at `acea3fb`); the test that reads the form follows.

## Risks, as reopened

**The exact-pixel promise and the minimal margin pull against each other**:
a module grid cannot hit every pixel size with a 4-module margin, so the
remainder lives in the margin, the drawn size, or a stated rounding. Which
one wins is the build's to argue and record — hiding it again is the
failure this reopening exists to end.

# Reopened again — 2026-08-12, second

**The owner used the size control that the first reopening shipped and rejected
its central behaviour** ([F221](../deferred-findings.md#open)): *"Setting the
resolution of the QR code jumps to a different number after it is set. The
number set is where it should stay, the quiet zone should be reduced to fit."*
Work below carries M49's number, as the first reopening's did. Reviewed by
[M57.9](m57.9.md).

**This reverses [D179](../decisions.md), which landed at `ad28c5f` the same
day.** That is not a correction of a mistake — D179 answered the question it was
asked, and the owner approved it — it is the owner seeing the shipped behaviour
and preferring the other side of the trade. The record says so in those terms or
it is lying about what happened.

## Done means, as reopened again

- **The requested size is the size stored and drawn, exactly, at every
  value in the range.** No snap, no notice, nothing to report — the
  `SizeFit.Snapped` path that the first reopening kept has nothing left to
  say and goes. The mechanism is that only the *symbol* needs whole
  modules: `size = modules·scale + 2·margin_px`, with the quiet zone
  carried in pixels rather than in modules.
- **The quiet zone lands inside 3 to 5 modules**, the ISO/IEC 18004
  minimum of four ±25%, chosen by picking the scale whose remainder puts
  the margin nearest four. Where the band cannot be held it is stated
  rather than silently broken: a 3-module zone on the widest symbol needs
  more pixels than `MinSize` currently admits, so either `MinSize` rises
  or the low end is documented as out of band — **whichever it is, a test
  asserts it and `docs/SECURITY.md` is not the place it is recorded.**
  *(Amended 2026-08-13 at step 3.4: the either/or was an incomplete
  enumeration and the build took a third answer — a floor that moves per
  code, `qr.MinSizeFor` = `2×(modules+6)`, 70px at 29 modules and 366px at
  version 40. `MinSize` does not rise, because raising it far enough to
  cover every symbol would cost five sixths of the range, and no size is
  documented out of band because none is admitted out of band. What the
  bullet asserts — the low end is handled and stated rather than silently
  broken — is what shipped.)*
- **A margin below the ISO floor is measured, not assumed.** Three modules
  is below the specification's four. `make verify-scan` exists and gates
  the logo fraction; the band's low end goes through it the same way,
  across the version range, and the result is what the number rests on.
- **`MaxSize` rises from 2000 to 2048**, owner-instructed, so the top
  slider stop is a round power of two. Everything derived from it moves
  with it and is restated where it is written down — `MaxScale`, the
  raster bound in `docs/SECURITY.md` and D142's allocation figures, and
  the openapi bounds.
- **The size control becomes a slider with snap points and an editable
  number.** Stops at 128, 256, 300, 512, 600, 1024, 1200 and 2048 — powers
  of two plus the three figures that matter at 300dpi — with the range
  ends reachable. The number beside it is clickable and accepts a typed
  value. No new script, `style-src 'self'` and `script-src 'self'`
  unchanged, and the control is asserted by a template test.

## Risks, as reopened again

**The band and the range fight at the bottom.** A 3-module quiet zone on a
169-module symbol at `MinScale` needs 350px, and `MinSize` is 64. Something
gives, and which one is the build's to argue: raising `MinSize` refuses sizes
the product accepts today, and opening the band at the low end means the owner's
±25% is not what ships. Neither is silent.

**A slider is a control this product has never drawn.** There is no precedent in
`input.css` for one, native styling differs across engines, and the click-to-type
number must not become a second source of truth for the same value.
