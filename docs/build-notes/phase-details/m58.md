## Risks

**The phase's budget is the risk.** Fifteen milestones with two reviews and this
close leaves twelve for work, and the plan spends all twelve. There is no slack
for a milestone that turns out to be two, and the most likely candidate is the
redesign — whose size was estimated before the walkthrough that specifies it.
If it grows, something moves to Phase 4 and that is a phase-boundary
conversation, not an insertion.

The second risk is the one M45 met: this milestone is where a phase's accumulated
small untruths come due, and there are more of them than usual because M52 alone
corrects five sites that describe erasure and M55 re-counts an enumeration that
has already been wrong once. Counting rather than trusting is the mechanism, and
it is stated because the alternative is discovering the count during the release.
